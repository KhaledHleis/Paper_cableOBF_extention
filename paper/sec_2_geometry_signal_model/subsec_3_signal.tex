\subsection{what the platform measures}\label{subsec:signal_model}

each body mounted sensor records the body frame vector : 

\begin{equation}
    \vect{F_b} = (\mat{I} + \mat{E_s})R^{nb}_{(Roll,Pitch,\chi)} (\vect{T}+\vect{B_s}+\vect{B_{ext}}) + \vect{h_s} + \vect{n_s}
    \label{eq:recorded_field}
\end{equation}

where $\vect{B_{ext}}$ is an external magnetic field that is not the cable anomaly, $\mat{E_s},\vect{h_s}$ are the soft iron effect matrix and the hard iron offset which represents here their residue after sensor calibration and the platform static field unaccounted for in that stage, $R^{nb}_{(Roll,Pitch,\chi)}$ is a Rotation matrix from the body frame to NED frame with $\chi$ denoting the yaw of the drone, to apply this rotation matrix the attitude have to be known, $\vect{n_s}$ is the sensor noise.

the recorded field is processed to give 3 main channels:

- Total-field $|\vect{F_b}|$ : this channel is the norm of the measured x,y,z components, its main advantage is its independance from the attitude, in result the errors of attitude estimation are abscent.
- Vector Components in NED: in this paper contorrary to [2]; the processing is in the NED frame, thus the recorded data us in a frame that is its origin is following the drone but its rotation is locked to the north and east of the regional field, the removal of the regional magnetic field is dependent on the attitude estimation which will leak an error from the regional field that is comparable to the magnitude of the measuremtns in  this channel, this effect will be tested in later sections.
- Gradiometer DUO-UNO: $\vect{T}$ and $\vect{B_{ext}}$ are common to both sensors and cancel, an attitude error then rotates only the diffrence vector but attitude is sitll needed to orient the measurements to calculate the diffrence in NED frame.

the total field needs no attitude but mixes the two phases(its shape changes with $\beta$), the vector components have pure phase (shape does not varie with $\beta$) but need attitude, the gradient have pure phase (also vector components) and needs only the heading from the attitude, its usage comes with the cost of an amplitude reduction by $b|\cos \theta | / \sqrt{2} d$. this tradoff organises every comparison that follows.
