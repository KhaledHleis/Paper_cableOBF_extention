\section*{Nomenclature}

Every symbol is defined again where it first appears, and this list gathers them with their units and the values used in the simulations.
Vectors are bold.
Horizontal positions are written (East, North) in metres, and three-dimensional vectors are in the local North--East--Down (NED) frame unless stated otherwise.
